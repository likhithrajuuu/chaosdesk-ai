# Password reset

Document ID: KB-001
Status: current

Applies to users who forgot their ChaosDesk password.

Steps:
1. Open the ChaosDesk sign-in page.
2. Select "Forgot password".
3. Enter the email address associated with the account.
4. Follow the instructions in the reset email.

If the email does not arrive, check the spam folder.
If the problem continues, contact support for investigation.

Support must never ask the customer to share their password.
